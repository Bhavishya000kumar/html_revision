Semantic & Visual Text Formatting

This example demonstrates the difference between semantic importance tags and visual formatting elements.

1. Semantic Importance vs Visual Bold

SECURITY WARNING: Do not share your account password with anyone! (Uses <strong> for high importance)

Key terms in this chapter are HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. (Uses <b> for visual bold without added weight)

2. Stress Emphasis vs Alternate Tone

You must submit your placement portfolio before Friday midnight. (Uses <em> for stress emphasis)

The Latin phrase cogito, ergo sum translates to "I think, therefore I am". (Uses <i> for foreign words)

3. Mathematical & Chemical Formulas (sub & sup)

Water Chemical Formula: H2O (Subscript <sub>)

Sulfuric Acid Formula: H2SO4

Pythagoras Theorem: a2 + b2 = c2 (Superscript <sup>)

Event Date: October 15th, 2026

4. Highlight, Fine Print & Annotations

Search query matches: JavaScript DOM course found in catalog. (Uses <mark> for highlight)

Unarticulated text annotation: Spelling mistake target (Uses <u>)

Terms & Conditions apply. Copyright © 2026 Bhavishya. All rights reserved. (Uses <small> for fine print)
